Hex houses

One L-shaped house at all twelve 30° angles on one empty terrain, with the hex grid showing. The house is its cells; the walls follow from them (WORLD.md § One game, square or hex).

  • terrain cells
  • the house's cells
  • the thin walls the rules use: the edges between inside and outside
  • a straight wall on the house's axes, as hex_draw places it
  • a straight wall off the house's axes: the open problem

Even angles are the grid's own edge directions, odd angles its corner directions: a 30° turn is no symmetry of the hex grid, so the two families give different cells. Each wall is hex_draw's surface over its run of edges: the sum of the edge vectors for its direction, the exact mean of their midpoints for its position, mitred where two walls meet.
