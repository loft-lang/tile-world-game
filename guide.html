Getting started

Any key skips the credits. You start in the demo village: each house shows one mechanic, its name written on the ground in front of it. Walk onto a door to go in or out; every place stays as you left it. The game explains nothing — watch what the world does; its rules hold everywhere.

Playing

KeysDoes
WASD or arrowsWalk. Walking into things pushes, opens, climbs and talks.
QTake a small item (walking over it does not), or put down what you hold. Held while walking, it pulls what is behind you.
EUse what you hold: swing it (hold the key for a heavy swing), shoot a bow, throw a bomb, drink a potion.
TabAim.
ShiftGuard: tap to parry, hold to block.
B BBack to the last well or fountain.
RRestart the place as it was when you came in.
MZoom in on the hero, and back out.
F1Switch to the first-person view and back (browser only).
F2Open the editor on the place you are in, and close it.
F3Roam free (an experiment): move smoothly, diagonals too, standing between tiles.
EscQuit (in a browser, reload to play again).

First person

F1 shows the same place from the hero's eyes, with what you hold in front of you.

KeysDoes
W or ↑Walk the way you look.
S or ↓Step back, still looking ahead. With Q held, you pull what is in front of you.
AD or ←→Turn left or right on the spot.
F1Back to the 2D view.

The other keys work as in 2D. Try it in CAVES (the village's south-east corner): a sword lies two steps into the tunnel.

Health and saving

  • The hearts at the bottom right are your health; falls and blows cost some.
  • Walking into a well or fountain saves; losing all health brings you back there.
  • The save stays in this browser. To start afresh, clear this site's data.
  • The gap in the village's east edge ends the game.

Editing

F2 edits the place you are in: a cursor, a palette along the bottom, and a panel on the right for the mouse. P play-tests your edits on a copy; F2 returns.

Desktop only for now: Shift+[ / ] lower or raise a ceiling, and Shift+/ turns a cut back.

The panel (with a mouse)

  • LEVEL — the place's name; SAVE, NEW, PLAY.
  • LEVELS — click one to open it.
  • BUILD — ROOM or HALL, then drag on the map. Click a room to pick it and switch its words (rubble…); FILL fills the place in, DROP removes the room. Hand-painted cells are kept.
  • DOOR TO — a place's name and SET make the cursor's cell a door there.
  • TEXT — words on the ground at the cursor. SERVES — the place's tags.

Where your work goes

Saved places stay in this browser (as levels/<name>.json) and survive a reload; there is no export yet. On the desktop they go into the game's levels/ folder.
