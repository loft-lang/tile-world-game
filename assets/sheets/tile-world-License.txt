
	tile-world sheet

	Drawn for the tile_world game, in the style of Kenney's Tiny Dungeon
	(www.kenney.nl), with Tiny Dungeon's own palette.  The staff in a socket
	(tile 12) uses Kenney's staff (Tiny Dungeon tile 129) as it is.
