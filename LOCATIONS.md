# The locations

Each place of the story game: what makes it unique, what can be found there, what the hero
learns there, and what the player can look out for.  In almost every place there are several
paths to take, with levels partly out of sight (owner, 2026-11-04).  So the hero can sneak past
almost everything, but it gets harder near any group (owner).  Gathered from STORY.md and DESIGN.md
(2026-11-04).  A placement marked ◇ was picked as the logical one; the owner has let the open items
be filled in that way (2026-11-04), so ◇ marks what is ours to change freely, everything else the
owner's outline fixes.

## 1. Orbury, the village (@ST-Setting)

- **Unique:** the people.  Talk, their routines, day and night matter most here.  The village is
  the tutorial, though never presented that way (owner, 2026-11-04): it holds the world's main
  mechanics on a smaller scale, and less interconnected.  It is a sandbox: the hero has only light
  reasons to snoop around, which will clearly not stop players doing so.  The villagers are grumpy
  about snooping, but enough aid to the village makes them look past it (owner, 2026-11-04).  Stones to push; a chest, which someone
  brought down to the inn's basement; a cart to push, on the rails that run from the mine through
  the Wardens' camp all the way to the village (owner, 2026-11-04).  A well with a pump, driven by
  small crystals with far less power, which the alchemist can fill himself (owner, 2026-11-04;
  the village's form of the mine's pump, @GM-Flooded).  The lift has no village form: it speaks for
  itself (owner, 2026-11-04).  A small grey rat in the cemetery by daylight, and one in the inn's
  basement (owner, 2026-11-04; Tiny Dungeon's 124, the second rat kind, @ST-Residents).  The hero
  can bring a torch into the village at night, but it only makes him seen, so getting into homes
  with one is suspect (owner, 2026-11-04): light gives him away, as it does to the Kharn.  Climbing
  gets into the closed houses, and one of them needs pulling (owner).  The rails have a switch; the
  way up to the Wardens' camp is uphill, so a cart pushed up it rolls back down to town (owner,
  2026-11-04; carts roll on slopes, built).  There are axes in the closed houses; the player can take
  one along, and nothing more is needed than that (owner, 2026-11-04).  There are trees around the
  place too (owner): with an axe, they give sticks (built).
- **Found:** Thornfield's barn, where the hero sleeps, his horse lent to the Dunns (@ST-Farm);
  Hollis's home, potions and the crafting lessons (@ST-Alchemist); Agnes's house beside the empty
  inn (@ST-Innkeeper); the abandoned smithy, made to work, where the artefact is crafted
  (@ST-Artefact); abandoned houses, one with rope (@GM-Flotilla); the cemetery (@ST-Founder).
- **Learned:** the town's state and the court trouble (Agnes); the debate (Wick against Agnes,
  @ST-Debate); the mine in outline, out of date (Garth, @ST-Foreman); the drought and the hunger
  (the Dunns); at night, the founder's story and the lake that drowned him.
- **Look out for:** a blunt sword in the smithy, to be crafted whole again there (owner,
  2026-11-04); who stands where, and when; locked houses that can still be entered — but the
  villagers do not agree with the hero plundering the remains: not for what is taken (a rope, say),
  but because an outsider sneaking into houses is not done, so they get far less accommodating once
  they learn of it (owner); the smithy is the exception, its reopening applauded (owner);
  the smithy's missing parts ◇; the material for the barricade: straight beams, as for the
  flotilla, so the beams that cross the lake also close the village entrance (owner, 2026-11-04;
  @ST-Climax, @GM-Flotilla).  The barricade growing at the entrance shows how ready the village
  is ◇.

## 2. The Wardens' camp, on the main path (@ST-Warden)

- **Unique:** the line nobody crosses.  No fighting: the guards block, and the only way past them
  is talk.
- **Found:** the decree, posted ◇ (a signpost tells when walked into); the closed main road the
  ore was carried out by, with the mine's rails running through the camp to the village (owner).
- **Learned:** the official side of the court (Voss, sergeant to sergeant); that the Wardens are in
  danger too.
- **Look out for:** a fellow sergeant's respect, the key to moving them before the climax.

## 3. The hidden path and the brook (@ST-YoungVillager)

- **Unique:** the daylight way in.  Water in a drought, crabs, quiet.
- **Found:** the crabs along the brook (@ST-Residents); the cave-in entrance into the Old Seam.
- **Learned:** that Lark knows what the adults do not; that the land still has water somewhere ◇
  (the brook as a thread of the climate quests, @ST-Outside).
- **Look out for:** Lark's trust, earned at Thornfield ◇.

## 4. The open hillside

- **Unique:** the night way in.  Exposed, dark, the way hard to find: light is a cost.
- **Found:** the same cave-in entrance, the way others found it.
- **Learned:** what the dark does; why a torch is a risk near the Kharn (@ST-Stealth).
- **Look out for:** a landmark to steer by in the dark ◇.

## The Old Seam, all levels

- **A dead body:** a less fortunate adventurer who fell, explicitly not killed by the enemies there
  (owner, 2026-11-04): a fall is the mine's danger that kills, readable from the body (DESIGN.md §
  The principle: a deep fall stays deadly).  Which level ◇: the second, below a broken walkway.

## 5. The Old Seam, first level (@ST-Cyclopes)

- **Unique:** the Kharn's home.  Stealth against sight by light, calls for aid, a tribe and not
  monsters.
- **Found:** the Kharn, their chief and Urgha; their dwindling prey; Sable at work by day
  (@ST-Rival); Odran, and later Tamsin, looking for the materials (@ST-Wizard, @ST-Aide); bombs
  left where the work was ◇ (@ST-Artefact: throughout the mine, wherever there was work).
- **Learned:** that the Kharn are people, with a language and a hunger; how near the attack is,
  read from how little prey is left (@ST-Climax).
- **Look out for:** the routes between the Kharn's points; what Urgha can understand; the first
  chest ◇.  The cave-in entrance is not directly connected to the Kharn's main tunnels: there is a
  walk before reaching them (owner, 2026-11-04), a quieter stretch first.

## 6. The Old Seam, second level (@GM-CartLift)

- **Unique:** the working mine.  Carts, rails, the lift, the pick, rubble, unloading places.
- **Found:** the cart lift; a cart and a pick ◇; rubble only a cart can clear; beams for the
  flotilla (@GM-Flotilla); bombs in the workings and the half-built extensions; a chest behind the
  rubble ◇.
- **Learned:** how the mine worked: Garth's knowledge made current.
- **Look out for:** the unloading places; a full cart's weight.

## 7. The Old Seam, the flooded lower tunnels (@GM-Flooded)

- **Unique:** the drowned depth.  The pump, the draining a charge at a time, the slow refill.
- **Found:** the pump and its depleted crystal; what the water hid, opening a charge at a time; the
  lift's lowest stop; bombs where the deepest work stopped; a chest in the deepest tunnel ◇.
- **Learned:** that the water seeps back by the day, so the work is done in time.
- **Look out for:** the spare crystal, in the mine's old stores, which Garth remembers in the wrong
  place ◇.

## 8. The Still Lake and its caves (@GM-Flotilla)

- **Unique:** the oldest water.  The flotilla, the slimes, the place the founder drowned.
- **Found:** the magical materials around the lake (@ST-Mine); the slimes' caves (@ST-Residents);
  an island chest ◇.
- **Learned:** what the materials are for (Odran, Tamsin); where the mine began (@ST-Founder).
- **Look out for:** enough straight beams and rope; the slimes' habits.

## 9. The High Diggings and their mine (@ST-Rival)

- **Unique:** the rival's ground.  Sable's obstructions and traps: runaway carts, blocked
  walkways.
- **Found:** Sable's spot, with his goods and rope; his traps; bombs in the new extensions; a chest
  Sable has not reached ◇.
- **Learned:** what Sable is after; that he cannot get home before dusk.
- **Look out for:** the hours he is away; what taking from him costs in talk afterwards.

## 10. Stormcap (@GM-Flooded)

- **Unique:** the open sky.  Weather, the lightning installation, bigfoot.
- **Found:** the installation; bigfoot, asleep at night in the start of a mine near the top
  (@ST-Residents); a chest in bigfoot's den ◇.
- **Learned:** how to catch the lightning; that Hollis can be brought up safely.
- **Look out for:** the weather that is needed; bigfoot's habits; a safe way for Hollis.

## Aid to the village, and the remainders

**Aid** wins back the villagers' goodwill after snooping, and brings people behind the barricade
(@ST-Climax).  From the start (owner): helping the farm directly; foraging food, meat and
vegetables, for the villagers.  More, picked as logical ◇:

- the village well's pump charged with Hollis's small crystals: water in the drought ◇;
- the smithy working again, applauded (owner), and what it can make for others ◇;
- tools for Garth's idle miners, found in the mine ◇;
- word of the Fenns, or the Fenns themselves once the threat wanes (@ST-Debate) ◇.

Meat comes from hunting, and every monster taken brings the Kharn's attack nearer, since their prey
is the clock (@ST-Climax): feeding the village and buying time pull against each other ◇.

**The remainders**, picked as logical ◇:

- **No save point in the village:** it is the safe place, and the first fountain lies where danger
  starts, at the cave-in entrance ◇ (@ST-Fountains).
- **The grey rat's own way:** it forages by daylight and carries off food left lying, so it
  competes with the hero's foraging ◇ (@ST-Residents).
- **The bow:** in one of the inn's rooms, left by a previous traveller; Agnes had better not see
  the hero taking it out of there, while the other villagers are less protective of travellers'
  goods (owner, 2026-11-04; @ST-Innkeeper).  Rennick, befriended, may part with one too ◇.
- **Money:** in this village ore stands for wealth, and its flow has dried up (owner, 2026-11-04).
  So the game's money is ore here ◇: found in the mine and the chests, of value to the villagers
  as the flow they lost, and to the contenders (Lord Garrick's interest is money).
- **Rennick's home:** not in the open, but in a small clump of woodland on the hillside, drawn
  with the tiles (owner, 2026-11-04); outside the village that cast him out.
- **The Fenns' empty house:** one of the closed houses in the village, the one with rope ◇.

## What this shows

- **The artefact gives the player something to look out for everywhere:** one chest per major
  place ◇ (the Old Seam's three levels, the lake, the High Diggings, Stormcap): six pieces, each
  behind that place's own mechanic.
- **Bombs belong to the working mine:** the Old Seam's levels and the High Diggings' extensions,
  not the village or the hills.
- **Save points:** the strange fountains, in the mines and the hills (@ST-Fountains); the village's
  well is a pump with small crystals, not one of them.
- **Still without a place:** the bow; money (what it buys); where Rennick lives; the Fenns' empty
  house.  (The axe is in the closed houses, the well in the village.)
- **Thin places:** the Wardens' camp and the hillside are crossings more than places to explore.
